Team Outings

A Team Outing near Kozhikode, in the Hills

Puduppadi, Thamarassery·About an hour from Kozhikode

Need a day out of the office that isn't another mall or resort buffet? Bring your team to Naz's Farm House, a private hill villa about an hour from Kozhikode. The whole place is yours for the day: garden, terrace, hall and games nook, with forested hills all around and no other guests.

The whole property, just for your team

Mon–Thu · 11 AM to 9 PM · up to 15 people

Book any Monday to Thursday and the villa is closed to everyone else that day. Arrive at 11, spend the afternoon in the garden or on the terrace, and stay for sunset over the valley before heading back down the hill by 9 PM.

What your team can do

Garden · terrace · hall · games nook

Hold a relaxed planning session in the double-height hall, then switch off: carrom and games in the games nook, music on the Bluetooth speaker, a walk around the garden, and tea on the terrace as the light goes. WiFi and on-site parking are included.

Food your way

Bring a caterer · delivery · BBQ grill (free)

Food isn't included, so you choose: bring your own caterer, order from restaurants nearby that deliver (vegetarian too), or use the full kitchen. The BBQ grill on the terrace is free to use (bring your own chicken or meat and do the prep), and it's the favourite way to end a team day.

Simple pricing

₹8,500 for the day · bigger teams on request

₹8,500 covers the whole property from 11 AM to 9 PM for up to 15 people. Bigger team? Send us your headcount and we'll quote. If some of you want to stay the night, book an overnight stay instead: the whole villa for up to 8 guests.

Getting here

~36 km from Kozhikode city · 6 km from Thamarassery

We're on NH 766 toward Wayanad, about 6 km past Thamarassery and just before the ghat starts to climb. From Kozhikode city it's roughly an hour's drive, so a team can leave after a short morning at work and still have a full afternoon here. More in our Kozhikode homestay guide.

Questions teams ask

How many people can come for a team outing?
Up to 15 guests for a weekday day outing. For a bigger team, message us with your headcount and we'll send a quote.
Which days and times are available?
Monday to Thursday, 11 AM to 9 PM. The whole property is booked for your team only, with no other guests that day.
How much does a team day cost?
₹8,500 for the whole property for the day, for up to 15 guests. Food isn't included; the BBQ grill is free to use.
Is food provided?
Food isn't included. You can bring your own caterer, order from nearby restaurants that deliver (vegetarian too), or use the kitchen. The BBQ grill on the terrace is free to use; just bring your own chicken or meat and do the prep yourselves.
How far is it from Kozhikode?
About an hour from Kozhikode city, up NH 766 toward Wayanad. The villa is in Puduppadi, about 6 km from Thamarassery, with parking on site.
Can the team stay overnight?
Yes, as a regular overnight booking: the whole villa for up to 8 guests, check-in after 2 PM and check-out before 11 AM. Day outings don't include an overnight stay.
