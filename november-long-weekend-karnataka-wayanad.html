Long Weekend Guide

The Last Long Weekend of November

Long Weekend Guide·27–29 November 2026

Kanakadasa Jayanti falls on Friday 27 November this year, and it's a holiday in Karnataka. That gives you Friday to Sunday off, the last long weekend before the December rush. It's a good one for a short trip with friends or family, before the year-end crowds and prices arrive.

Why late November

Cool, clear and quieter

By the end of November the rains are well behind us. Mornings in the hills are cool and misty, afternoons are clear, and the evenings are good for sitting out on the terrace. Wayanad hasn't hit its December peak yet, so the viewpoints and waterfalls are calmer.

A private villa for your group

One group at a time, no other guests

Naz's Farm House is booked by one group at a time. You get two bedrooms, The Majlis loft for extra sleepers, a double-height hall, a full kitchen, a garden and a terrace over forested hills. A BBQ evening on the terrace can be arranged for an extra charge, and restaurants nearby deliver a good variety of food, vegetarian included.

Getting here

Bangalore: roughly 320 km · Mysore: roughly 175 km

Leave Bangalore early on Friday and take NH766 through Mysore, Gundlupet and Bandipur into Wayanad, then down the Thamarassery ghat to us. The Bandipur forest stretch is closed from 9 PM to 6 AM, so time the drive to cross it in daylight. Our Bangalore weekend route guide has the full timing. Flying in? Calicut International Airport is about 46 km away.

Three easy days

Waterfalls, viewpoints and the sea

Friday: Arrive by evening and watch the sun go down over the valley from the terrace. Saturday: An early start for Thusharagiri Falls, then Amarad Waterfalls 9 km from the house, and a lazy afternoon back at the villa. Sunday: Drive up the ghat to Lakkidi View Point on your way home, or go down to Kozhikode for a biriyani lunch and the beach. Our beach guide covers the best ones.

For friends and families

Carrom, karaoke and a loft sleepover

The house is as good for staying in as going out. There's a carrom board in the games nook, a Bluetooth speaker for music, a swing, and The Majlis loft for a floor sleepover. The kitchen is yours to use, with a fridge, kettle and sandwich maker.

Planning the 27–29 November weekend?

Long weekends book out first, and we host only one group at a time. Check the calendar, then message us on WhatsApp for availability and rates.
