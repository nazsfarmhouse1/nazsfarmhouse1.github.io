Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

What's included when we book?
A booking is for the whole property — both bedrooms, the double-height hall, and The Majlis loft — not a single room. It's yours for the night.
How much does a stay cost?
Stays start from ₹8,500 per night for the whole property. The final rate is confirmed when you send an inquiry, since it depends on your dates and number of guests.
What are the check-in and check-out times?
Check-in and check-out are flexible by arrangement — let us know your travel plans when you inquire and we'll work around them.
Are pets allowed?
Yes, pets are welcome at Naz's Farm House.
Is there a kitchen we can cook in?
Yes, the property has a self-catered kitchen guests are free to use during their stay.
Is parking available on site?
Yes, on-site parking is available for guests.
Do you host weddings and events?
Yes — the garden and outdoor stage host destination weddings and celebrations for 200–250 guests, and the indoor hall and Majlis loft suit smaller family gatherings of 15–20. Select "An event / function" on the inquiry form above to get started.
Where exactly is Naz's Farm House located?
We're at 8 Acre Road, Anganavadi Vanaboomi, Puduppadi, Thamarassery, Kerala 673586 — on the Kozhikode side of the ghat road toward Wayanad, about 6 km from Thamarassery.
How do I confirm a booking?
Send an inquiry through the form or WhatsApp with your dates and headcount. Submitting the form doesn't confirm your dates by itself — we'll follow up by phone or WhatsApp to finalize everything.
