Celebrations

A Birthday in the Hills

Celebrations·Birthdays near Kozhikode

A birthday at home is lovely, but someone always ends up cooking and cleaning. A birthday at a restaurant is easy, but it's over in two hours. A private villa sits in between: the whole house is yours for the day or the night, the kids have a garden to run around in, and the grown-ups get to sit on a terrace looking at forested hills. Naz's Farm House is about an hour from Kozhikode, at the foot of the Wayanad ghat, and birthdays are one of the things families book it for most.

What a birthday here looks like

One group at a time · the whole villa is yours

Recently, a family of seven came for two nights to celebrate their little girl's 4th birthday. They decorated the hall, grilled chicken on the terrace at night, played board games and badminton, and woke up to misty hills. In their review they called it "a wonderful place to relax and spend quality time with family". That's the idea: no other guests, no time slot, just your family and the day.

Overnight stay or a day party

Stay: up to 8 guests · Weekday day event: up to 15 guests

Overnight: book the villa for one or more nights and up to 8 guests can stay, with check-in from 2 PM and check-out by 11 AM. That's enough for a family birthday with grandparents and cousins. Day party: on weekdays (Monday to Thursday) you can book the villa from 11 AM to 10 PM for up to 15 guests, and 4 or 5 of you can stay the night afterwards. For bigger gatherings, ask us on WhatsApp.

The space

Hall · garden · terrace · The Majlis loft

The double-height hall is the heart of the house and the natural spot for the cake and the decorations. Outside there's a fenced garden lawn and a terrace under the bougainvillea, with the hills on every side. Two bedrooms with attached bathrooms are on the ground floor, and The Majlis loft upstairs is where the kids usually want to sleep.

Food and cake

Full kitchen · BBQ grill free to use

Bring your cake and snacks from a bakery in Thamarassery or Kozhikode on the way. The kitchen is fully equipped if you want to cook, and the BBQ grill on the terrace is free to use: bring your own chicken or meat and our caretaker will help you get it going. If you'd rather not cook at all, restaurants nearby deliver with their own delivery staff. A simple Kerala breakfast from a local caterer can be arranged the next morning if you order a day ahead.

Keeping the kids busy

Carrom · board games · badminton · karaoke

There's a carrom board, board games and playing cards, badminton rackets for the lawn, a karaoke set and a party speaker for the birthday playlist. The swing is always a hit. For the smallest guests, a baby cradle is available on request, and the garden is fenced.

A few house rules

Quiet hours from 10 PM

Music and decorations are welcome. We just ask that music is turned down at 10 PM out of respect for our neighbours, and smoking isn't allowed.

Getting here

Kozhikode ~1 hr · Thamarassery ~6 km · Calicut airport ~46 km

The villa is in Puduppadi, about 6 km from Thamarassery towards Wayanad, and the road is tarred right up to the house. Parking is free, with space for up to 8 cars. Coming with grandparents? Our guide for families with elders and little ones covers the details. For a work celebration instead, see our events page.

Planning a birthday?

We host one group at a time, so weekend dates go first. Send us the date, the number of guests and whether you want to stay the night, and we'll confirm on WhatsApp.
