Long Weekend Guide

Sankranti in the Hills

Long Weekend Guide·15–17 January 2027

Sankranti falls on a Friday in 2027, which turns 15 to 17 January into a three-day weekend for a lot of Bangalore and Mysore families. The Christmas crowds have gone home by then, the hills are still cool and misty in the mornings, and the drive through Bandipur and down the Wayanad ghat is one of the best in the south. At the bottom of that ghat, on the Kozhikode side, is our private villa.

The dates

Sankranti: Fri 15 Jan · long weekend Fri 15 – Sun 17 Jan

Sankranti is on Friday 15 January 2027, and Pongal in Tamil Nadu falls on the same day. Leave on Thursday evening or Friday morning, spend two nights with us, and head home on Sunday after breakfast. Holiday calendars vary by employer and school, so check yours first.

The drive from Bangalore and Mysore

Bangalore ~320 km, 7–8 hrs · Mysore roughly 185 km

Take the Mysore road, then NH766 through Nanjangud and Gundlupet, across Bandipur into Kerala at Muthanga, and on through Sulthan Bathery and Kalpetta. After Lakkidi at the top of the ghat, it's the nine hairpin bends of the Thamarassery ghat down to us at Puduppadi. Mysore makes an easy breakfast stop. Our Bangalore weekend route guide has the full timing.

Time Bandipur right

NH766 through Bandipur: closed to cars 9 PM – 6 AM

The forest stretch through Bandipur Tiger Reserve is closed overnight. On a long weekend the road is busier than usual, so leave Bangalore by 6 or 7 AM and you'll cross in daylight with time to spare. On the way home, check out by 11 AM and you'll be through Bandipur well before the gates close.

Two days at the villa

The whole villa · up to 8 guests

The house is yours alone: two bedrooms with attached bathrooms on the ground floor, The Majlis loft for extra sleepers, a double-height hall, a full kitchen, and a garden terrace looking out over forested hills. Spend the first evening on the terrace for the sunset, and use the BBQ grill, which is free (bring your own chicken or meat). Carrom, board games and badminton keep everyone busy after dark.

What to do nearby

Waterfalls · the ghat · the Malabar coast

Thusharagiri Falls are about 19 km away; get there for the 8 AM opening before the crowds. Amarad Waterfalls are closer still. Or drive down to Kozhikode (about an hour) for a beach walk and a proper Malabar biriyani lunch, then come back up for an evening in the hills. Our beach guide lists the best stretches of sand.
