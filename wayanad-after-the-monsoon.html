Season Guide

Wayanad After the Monsoon

Season Guide·Naz's Farm House

Kerala sits toward the end of the monsoon's retreat, so the rain here lingers longer than it does further north — but once it clears, this corner of the Western Ghats moves into its best stretch of the year. Here's what actually changes once the monsoon eases.

The rain starts to ease

Late September into October

The southwest monsoon typically withdraws from the country only after October 1st, and Kerala — being part of the southern peninsula — is usually among the last regions it leaves, often not fully clearing until well into October. In practice that means the heaviest, most constant rain is behind us by late September, even if the odd shower still passes through into October.

Waterfalls are still full, trails are drying out

Thusharagiri and beyond

This is the sweet spot: the waterfalls are still running full from the season's rain, but the trek paths are firm enough to actually walk again. Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary's entry points, which stay closed for part of the monsoon, reopen for regular visits around this time too — worth checking current hours before you go.

Clearer skies, cooler nights

Peak season: October–February

October through February is when this part of Kerala is at its most comfortable — clear ghat-road views, cooler evenings, and none of the humidity that makes April and May harder going. It's also, unsurprisingly, when the property books up fastest.

Worth planning ahead for

Book before the peak fills up

If a stay here is on the radar for the coming months, this is the window to lock in dates before the good weekends go first — especially anything close to a long weekend or a festival date.

Plan your stay for the clear season

Whole-property stays from ₹8,500/night — check dates before the peak-season weekends fill up.

Check Availability

Monsoon withdrawal timing follows the India Meteorological Department's general pattern for the season and can shift year to year — check current conditions before travel, especially for wildlife sanctuary entry or trekking permissions.
