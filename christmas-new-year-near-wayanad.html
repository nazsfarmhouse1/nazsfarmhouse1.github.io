Holiday Guide

Christmas & New Year in the Hills

Holiday Guide·December 2026 – January 2027

This year Christmas Day falls on a Friday, and so does New Year's Day 2027. That gives you two easy long weekends back to back, with school holidays in between. If the family wants to be together somewhere cool and quiet, without sharing a resort with strangers, a private villa at the foot of the Wayanad ghat is a good place to do it.

The holiday dates

Christmas: Fri 25 Dec · New Year's Eve: Thu 31 Dec · New Year's Day: Fri 1 Jan

Christmas weekend runs Friday 25 to Sunday 27 December. New Year runs from Thursday 31 December to Sunday 3 January, four days if you can take Thursday off. For Kerala state schools, the 2026–27 academic calendar (as published by education sites) puts the Christmas holidays at 18 to 28 December, so families can travel in the week before Christmas too. CBSE and other schools set their own dates, so check yours.

December in the hills

Misty mornings, clear afternoons, cool nights

December is dry season in Wayanad. Mornings start cold and misty, the sky clears by mid-morning, and the air cools down again by late afternoon. Bring a light jacket or shawl for the mornings and evenings, especially for grandparents and small children. Down at our level, at the foot of the ghat, it's a little milder than up in Wayanad itself, which makes it comfortable for older guests.

One house, the whole family

Up to 8 guests · one group at a time

Naz's Farm House is booked by one group at a time. You get both bedrooms with attached bathrooms (both on the ground floor), The Majlis loft for extra sleepers, a double-height hall to gather in, a full kitchen, and a fenced garden and terrace over forested hills. Travelling with grandparents? Our guide for families with elders and little ones covers the details, from the European toilets to the 24-hour hospital 1.4 km away.

Christmas at the villa

Your own tree, your own feast

Bring a small tree, stars and fairy lights and make the hall your own. The kitchen is fully equipped if you want to cook the Christmas lunch yourselves, and restaurants nearby deliver with their own delivery staff if you'd rather not. In the evening, the BBQ grill on the terrace is free to use: bring your own chicken or meat and do the prep yourselves. Plum cake from a Kozhikode bakery on the way up is a good idea too.

A quiet New Year's Eve

Quiet hours from 10 PM

New Year's Eve here is for families and close friends, not big parties. Gather on the terrace for an early dinner off the grill, play carrom or cards in the games nook, and count down indoors with your own group. Our quiet hours start at 10 PM, out of respect for the neighbours, so music needs to be turned down by then. If you want a calm start to 2027 with the hills around you, this is it. If you want a DJ night, it's not the right place.

Days out from the villa

Waterfalls, the ghat and the sea, all within an hour

Amarad Waterfalls are about 9 km away, a short forest walk to the falls. Thusharagiri Falls are just up the road. Drive up the ghat to Lakkidi View Point (about 45 minutes) for the view back over the hairpin bends, and go early, as Wayanad is at its busiest in the holiday weeks. Or head down to the coast: Kozhikode Beach is about 55 minutes away for a sunset walk and a biriyani dinner. Our beach guide lists the best ones.

Getting here

Calicut airport ~46 km · Kozhikode station ~37 km · Mysore roughly 185 km

Coming from Bangalore or Mysore, take NH766 through Bandipur into Wayanad and down the Thamarassery ghat. The Bandipur forest stretch is closed to traffic from 9 PM to 6 AM, so plan to cross it in daylight. Our Bangalore weekend route guide has the timing. Flying in for the holidays? Calicut International Airport is about 1 hr 20 min away. From Kochi, see our Kochi weekend guide.
