Stay near Wayanad

A Private Homestay near Wayanad

Puduppadi, Thamarassery·Foot of the Wayanad ghat

Naz's Farm House is a private homestay near Wayanad, right where the Thamarassery ghat starts its climb into the hills. You get the whole villa, forested hills on every side and a terrace made for slow evenings, with Wayanad's lakes and viewpoints an easy morning drive up the road.

The whole villa, just for your group

Up to 8 guests · one group at a time

This isn't a room in a resort. When you book, the whole property is yours: the Garden View and Valley View bedrooms, The Majlis loft for extra sleepers, a double-height hall, a full kitchen, the garden and the terrace. No other guests, no shared spaces. It suits families, groups of friends and couples who want somewhere quiet.

How close is Wayanad?

Lakkidi ~45 min · Pookode Lake ~1 hr

We're on the Kozhikode–Wayanad road at the bottom of the ghat's nine hairpin bends. Drive up and you reach Lakkidi, the gateway to Wayanad, in roughly 45 minutes, then Vythiri and Pookode Lake shortly after. Thusharagiri Falls and Amarad Waterfalls are closer still, on our side of the ghat. Our things to do near Wayanad guide lists the best stops with distances.

Why stay below the ghat?

Quieter, and two trips in one

Wayanad town fills up fast on weekends. Staying at the foot of the ghat gives you the same hills and cool evenings without the traffic, and puts Kozhikode's beaches and famous food on the other side, about an hour down the hill (more on our homestay in Kozhikode page). Driving from Bangalore or Mysore, you arrive after the ghat while it's still light and head up to Wayanad fresh the next morning. See our Bangalore weekend route for timings.

What you get

Hill views · kitchen · garden

Two bedrooms with AC, The Majlis loft with floor mattresses, free WiFi, hot water, on-site parking and a carrom and games nook. The kitchen is yours to cook in. Food isn't included, but nearby restaurants deliver (vegetarian too), and a BBQ evening on the terrace can be arranged for an extra charge. Pets are welcome.

Getting here

Calicut airport ~46 km · Kozhikode station ~37 km

From Calicut International Airport it's about 1 hr 20 min by taxi, and Kozhikode railway station is about 1 hr 15 min away. By road from Bangalore (roughly 320 km) or Mysore (roughly 175 km), come via NH 766 through Bandipur and down the ghat. The Bandipur forest stretch is closed to traffic from 9 PM to 6 AM, so plan to cross it in the day.

Questions guests ask

How far is Naz's Farm House from Wayanad?
We're at the foot of the Thamarassery ghat on the Kozhikode–Wayanad road. Lakkidi, the gateway to Wayanad at the top of the ghat, is roughly 45 minutes' drive, and Vythiri and Pookode Lake are about an hour away.
Is it a whole-property booking or a room?
The whole villa is booked by one group at a time: both bedrooms, The Majlis loft, the double-height hall, the kitchen, the garden and the terrace. Up to 8 guests can stay overnight.
How much does it cost?
Stays start from ₹8,500 per night for the whole villa. The final rate is confirmed when you send an inquiry, as it depends on your dates and number of guests.
Is food included?
Food isn't included. You have a full kitchen to cook in, and nearby restaurants deliver, including vegetarian options. A BBQ evening on the terrace can be arranged for an extra charge.
Why stay below the ghat instead of in Wayanad town?
You get the hills and the quiet without the crowds, Kozhikode's beaches and food are on the other side, and coming from Bangalore or Mysore you can arrive after the ghat in daylight and head up to Wayanad fresh the next morning.
What are the check-in and check-out times?
Check-in is any time after 2:00 PM and check-out any time before 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out can often be arranged on request. See our Guest Guidelines.

Planning a Wayanad trip?

Whole-villa stays from ₹8,500/night for up to 8 guests. Check the calendar, then message us on WhatsApp to confirm your dates.
