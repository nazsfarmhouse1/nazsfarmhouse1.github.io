Staying In

Self-Catered Kitchen & BBQ Nights

Staying In·Naz's Farm House

Not every good part of a trip happens out on the road. Some of it happens right here — a slow breakfast you made yourself, a fire going in the evening with nowhere to be. The kitchen and the outdoor grill are what make that possible.

A kitchen that's actually yours

Self-catered, not room service

There's a full kitchen with a fridge, an electric kettle, a sandwich maker, and a dish rack — set up for you to cook, not just reheat. No fixed meal times, no set menu. Pick up groceries in Thamarassery town on the way in, or send word ahead on WhatsApp and we'll point you to what's nearby.

A Kerala breakfast, your own pace

Puttu, appam, dosa — whatever you feel like

This is the part most resorts can't offer: a proper Kerala breakfast made in your own time, at your own table, exactly how you like it — not a buffet on someone else's clock. Coffee first, then whatever the morning calls for.

Grill under the stars at night

Outdoor charcoal BBQ grill

Once the sun's down, the grill stand on the terrace takes over — charcoal, good company, and the hills going dark around you. No restaurant reservation, no last call. It ends when you decide it ends.

Everything washed, put away, ready again

Full kitchen kit

Dishes go straight into the rack, and the kitchen's ready for the next round — whether that's a second breakfast attempt or a midnight tea. It's built for actually living in, not just visiting.

See the kitchen and the grill for yourself

Both are part of every stay — no add-on, no separate charge. Whole-property stays from ₹8,500/night.
