Weddings & Functions

A Garden Wedding Venue in Kozhikode

Thamarassery · Kozhikode·50–60 guests

Looking for a small wedding venue in Kozhikode that isn't a banquet hall? Naz's Farm House is a private hillside farmhouse in Puduppadi, Thamarassery, with a garden stage for 50–60 guests, forested hills as the backdrop, and rooms so close family can stay over. It's booked by one family at a time, so the whole place is yours for the day.

Built for smaller weddings and functions

Nikah · Engagement · Mehendi · Reception

The venue suits gatherings where you want your close circle, not a hall full of strangers: a nikah or small wedding, an engagement, a mehendi or haldi night, a reception, a baby shower or a milestone birthday. For smaller family get-togethers of 15–20, the double-height hall and The Majlis loft work indoors.

The garden, stage and terrace

Up to 50–60 guests outdoors

The lawn opens up for the ceremony and seating, with the garden stage framed by coconut palms and forest. The terrace looks out over the hills, a natural spot for photos at sunset. You won't need much décor to make it look good; the setting does a lot of the work.

Your caterer, your decorator

No fixed packages

There's no house menu or in-house decorator to work around. Bring the caterer and decorator you trust, or ask us and we'll put you in touch with local vendors we know. A BBQ evening on the terrace can also be arranged for an extra charge.

Family can stay the night

Whole-property booking

Unlike a hall you hand back at the end of the evening, the farmhouse has bedrooms, The Majlis loft and a full kitchen. Close family can arrive the day before, get ready on site and stay on after the function.

Easy to reach

36 km from Kozhikode · 46 km from Calicut airport

We're on the Kozhikode–Wayanad road at the foot of the Thamarassery ghat: about an hour from Kozhikode town, 37 km from Kozhikode railway station and around 1 hr 20 min from Calicut International Airport. Guests coming from Wayanad, Mukkam, Koduvally or Thamarassery town are even closer.

Frequently asked questions

How many guests can the venue hold?

Up to 50–60 guests outdoors in the garden, and 15–20 for a smaller indoor gathering in the hall and The Majlis.

Where is the venue?

Puduppadi, Thamarassery, in Kozhikode district — about 36 km from Kozhikode town, 37 km from Kozhikode railway station and 46 km from Calicut International Airport.

Can we bring our own caterer and decorator?

Yes. Décor and catering are arranged by you, with your own vendors. If you need suggestions, we can put you in touch with local caterers and decorators we know.

Can the family stay overnight?

Yes. The venue is a private farmhouse booked as a whole property, so close family can stay the night before or after the function.

What events do you host?

Small weddings, nikah ceremonies, engagements, mehendi and haldi nights, receptions, baby showers and birthday parties.

Until what time can music play?

Quiet hours start at 10 PM, so music and the main celebration should finish by then.
