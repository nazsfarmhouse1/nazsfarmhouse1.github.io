Stay in Kozhikode

A Hill Homestay in Kozhikode, near Thamarassery

Puduppadi, Thamarassery·Kozhikode district

Looking for a homestay in Kozhikode that feels like the hills, not the city? Naz's Farm House is a private villa in Puduppadi, about 6 km from Thamarassery, with forested hills on every side. It's close enough to Kozhikode for an easy drive, and quiet enough that you'll forget the traffic.

A homestay near Thamarassery

~6 km from Thamarassery town

We're on the road from Thamarassery toward Wayanad, just before the ghat starts to climb. Thamarassery town is about 15 minutes away for groceries, fuel and food, and Amarad Waterfalls at Kattippara is about 9 km. Thusharagiri Falls is about 19 km. Directions are on our contact page, and guests get a pinned location on WhatsApp.

A homestay near Kozhikode city

Kozhikode city ~36 km · about an hour

From Kozhikode city it's roughly an hour's drive up NH 766. That makes us an easy weekend escape for Kozhikode families, and a calmer base for visitors who want the city's beaches and food without staying in town. Our Kozhikode beaches guide covers the best ones for a day trip down the hill.

The whole villa, just for your group

Up to 8 guests · one group at a time

Most homestays rent rooms. Here the whole property is yours: the Garden View and Valley View bedrooms with AC, The Majlis loft for extra sleepers, a double-height hall, a full kitchen, the garden and a terrace looking over the hills. No shared spaces, no other guests.

What's included, and what isn't

Kitchen · WiFi · parking · pets welcome

Free WiFi, hot water, on-site parking, a carrom and games nook, and a full kitchen you can cook in. Food isn't included, but restaurants nearby deliver (vegetarian too), and the BBQ grill on the terrace is free to use (bring your own chicken or meat and do the prep). Check-in is after 2 PM and check-out before 11 AM.

Stays, day visits and small functions

Overnight · weekday day events · weddings

Besides overnight stays, you can book the whole property for a weekday day event (Monday to Thursday, 11 AM to 10 PM, up to 15 guests), and the garden hosts small weddings, nikah and engagements for 50–60 guests. Planning a short break from town? See our staycation ideas in Kozhikode.

Questions guests ask

Where exactly is the homestay?
8 Acre Road, Anganavadi Vanaboomi, Puduppadi, Thamarassery, Kozhikode, Kerala 673586. It's about 6 km from Thamarassery town on the road toward Wayanad, near the Perumpally junction.
How far is it from Kozhikode city?
Kozhikode city and the beach are about 36 km, roughly an hour's drive. Kozhikode railway station is about 37 km (about 1 hr 15 min) and Calicut International Airport about 46 km (about 1 hr 20 min).
Do you rent single rooms?
No. The whole villa is booked by one group at a time: both bedrooms, The Majlis loft, the hall, the kitchen, the garden and the terrace. Up to 8 guests can stay overnight.
How much does it cost?
Stays start from ₹8,500 per night for the whole villa. The final rate is confirmed when you send an inquiry, as it depends on your dates and number of guests.
Is food included?
Food isn't included. You have a full kitchen to cook in, and nearby restaurants deliver, including vegetarian options. The BBQ grill on the terrace is free to use; just bring your own chicken or meat and do the prep yourselves.
Can we book just for the day?
Yes. On weekdays (Monday to Thursday) you can book the whole property for the day, 11 AM to 10 PM, for up to 15 guests. See Events for details.
